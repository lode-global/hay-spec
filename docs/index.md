<a class="lode-guide-card" href="guides/crop_management.html"><h2>In-Season Crop Management</h2><p>This section documents how the hay crop was managed during the growing season - including cutting strategy, weed and pest management, and other contextual information such as weather stresses or field notes.</p></a>
